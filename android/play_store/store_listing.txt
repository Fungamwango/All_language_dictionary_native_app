=== APP NAME (max 30) ===
All Languages Dictionary

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80) ===
English into 100+ languages with examples, quiz, AI tutor and translator.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
Learn English words in your own language with a dictionary built for learners, teachers, travellers and anyone who wants to speak a new language with confidence.

7 FULL DICTIONARIES WITH EXAMPLES
• Bemba, Nyanja, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Swahili and Zulu
• Over 5,000 English words in each language
• Every word comes with an example sentence in English and in your language
• Search in English or in your language – suggestions appear as you type
• Listen to entries read aloud with your phone's voice, even offline

100+ MORE LANGUAGES
• Choose any of over 100 other languages and look up English words in it when you are online
• Change language any time from the top of the page

WORKS OFFLINE
• The 7 full dictionaries are inside the app – no data needed to look up words
• The quiz works without internet too

QUIZ
• 10-question quizzes from English to your language and back
• Earn points and move up through the levels
• Challenge friends and climb the leaderboard

AI TUTOR
• Ask questions about words, meanings and grammar
• Get answers in English or in your language

MORE
• Translator between English and many languages
• Bemba dictionary ebook to keep

The app is free with ads and daily free limits. Unlimited access and the Bemba dictionary ebook can be bought in the app.
